DNS Configuration Guide

Connecting gennetz.com in Namecheap

Follow this 3-minute guide to configure your Namecheap DNS records and link your domain to the new live website.

1
Log in to Namecheap & Open "Domain List"

1. Open namecheap.com and log in with your account credentials.
2. Click on Domain List in the left sidebar.
3. Locate gennetz.com and click the black/orange MANAGE button on the right.

Namecheap Dashboard → Domain List
gennetz.com

Status: Active

MANAGE
2
Navigate to the "Advanced DNS" Tab

Inside the domain management screen, click on the Advanced DNS tab at the top.

Domain
Products
Sharing & Transfer
Advanced DNS
Under this tab, you will see the HOST RECORDS table where we will add our records.
⚠️
Important: Check Nameservers
Under the Domain tab, ensure NAMESERVERS is set to Namecheap BasicDNS. (If it is set to Custom DNS, the Advanced DNS tab will not work).
3
Delete Default Parking Records (If Present)

Namecheap often puts default records for new domains (e.g. URL Redirect Record pointing to parking pages or default parking IP).
If you see any URL Redirect Record or old parking A Record, click the Trash Can (Delete) icon next to them to remove them.

TypeHostValueTTL
URL Redirect Record@http://www.gennetz.comUnmasked DELETE
CNAME Recordwwwparkingpage.namecheap.comAutomatic DELETE
4
Add the 2 New Records

Click the red/orange ADD NEW RECORD button at the bottom of the table twice to add the two records below:

TypeHostValueTTLSave
A Record @ 216.198.79.1 Automatic ✔
CNAME Record www 210bd603a9446013.vercel-dns-017.com Automatic ✔

👉 Remember: Click the small green checkmark (✔) on the right side of each row to save the record!

5
Verification & Live Deployment

1. Once you click save in Namecheap, DNS propagation begins automatically.
2. Go back to your Vercel Dashboard → Project Settings → Domains.
3. Click the Refresh button next to gennetz.com.
4. In 1 to 5 minutes, the red warning will turn into a Blue/Green Checkmark (Valid Configuration) and a free SSL certificate will be issued automatically.
